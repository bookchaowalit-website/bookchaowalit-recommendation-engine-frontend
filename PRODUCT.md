## Current product truth

- A client-side tag-overlap recommender for five authored sample items.
- The visitor toggles interest tags; the list re-ranks by matching tag count.
- State is held in the browser for the current session; there is no model,
  backend, account, remote catalog, or multi-tenant claim.
- The surface must make the ranking mechanism legible within seconds and label
  the demo boundary honestly.
